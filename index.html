Tips for good detection

  • Use the telephoto lens or zoom until each hole is at least 8–10 px wide. The status line shows this after locking on.
  • Holes in the black only show when light comes through them. A small light behind the target makes a big difference.
  • Mount the phone firmly. Auto mode waits for the view to settle before scoring.
  • Anything missed or misplaced: drag the marker, or use ＋ Add and Delete.
